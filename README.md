# Mobile Mirror

**Mobile Mirror** is a free, open-source desktop application for **Windows, macOS, and Linux** that lets you mirror your **Android** or **iPhone / iPad** screen and audio directly to your computer and record crystal-clear MP4 videos with a single click.

---

## What It Does

- **Instant Screen Mirroring**: View your Android phone (via USB) or iPhone / iPad (via local AirPlay or USB) live on your computer screen in real time with smooth high-frame-rate playback.
- **Audio & Microphone Capture**: Stream your phone's internal audio to your computer speakers and optionally capture your voice through your phone's microphone for tutorials, walkthroughs, or presentations.
- **One-Click MP4 Recording**: Record your mirrored phone screen and audio directly to standard `.mp4` video files with zero watermarks, zero time limits, and no quality loss.
- **100% Local & Private**: Works completely offline over your USB cable or local home Wi-Fi network—no accounts, no cloud servers, and no subscriptions required.

---

## Who It Is For

- **Content Creators & Educators** recording mobile app tutorials, gameplay, or voiceover walkthroughs.
- **Developers & Designers** demonstrating or testing mobile apps live on a desktop monitor.
- **Everyday Users** who want a simple, lightweight way to view and record their phone screen on a PC, Mac, or Linux machine.
